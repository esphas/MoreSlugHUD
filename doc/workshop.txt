添加了多项实用 HUD。

[url=https://github.com/esphas/MoreSlugHUD]Github[/url]

[h1]功能[/h1]

[h2]库存[/h2]

显示当前双手、腹部、背部的物品，此外：
[list]
  [*] 支持显示待拾取的候选物品
  [*] [饕餮,工匠] 支持显示合成结果预测
  [*] [工匠] 支持显示炸弹跳剩余可用热度
[/list]

在拓展菜单中可以自定义屏幕坐标及栏位布局。


[h2]生物标签[/h2]

在特定生物（蜥蜴、拾荒者、猫崽）头顶展示标签信息，包括：
[list]
  [*] 数字编号
  [*] 辨识名：使用古代符文取的名字
  [*] 意图（默认关闭）：用图标表示中立、友善与敌对关系
[/list]

在拓展菜单中可以自定义各项目是否展示。


[h2]输入历史[/h2]

实时展示一个（类似格斗游戏的）带帧数的玩家输入和运动状态的历史日志，记录项目有：
[list]
  [*] 运动方向：八方向+中立
  [*] 动作：抓取、跳跃、投掷、特殊
  [*] 状态：站立、爬行、空中、爬杆……
[/list]

在拓展菜单中可以配置各项目是否需要统计。

默认使用字母来表示各运动状态和动作，也可切换为图标。
（配置面板中有图例，目前的这套图标是我手绘的，我对其并不满意，欢迎提交改进。）


[h2]杂项[/h2]

可在[url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3458119961]键位设置[/url]中配置按键，以在游戏中切换面板的显示状态。
库存、生物标签无默认绑定按键；输入历史默认绑定键盘 [b]H[/b]、手柄 [b]L3[/b]。

联机兼容性：本地模组，不影响联机。


[h1]致谢[/h1]

本模组受到以下模组的启发：
[list]
  [*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2957745837]What's in My Pocket?[/url]：事实上本模组的起点就是“我想把它待办里说的合成预测做出来”
  [*] [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2934997065]Visible ID[/url]：这个模组解决了我“认不出这个蜥蜴是不是我驯化的那只”的问题，但由于它比较倾向于调试用途，对我而言暴露了太多信息（启用它之后我再也没被落网虫和拟态草偷袭成功过）
[/list]

--- en

Adds several practical HUDs.

[url=https://github.com/esphas/MoreSlugHUD]GitHub[/url]


[h1]Features[/h1]

[h2]Inventory[/h2]

Shows what you are holding in both hands, stomach, and back, plus:
[list]
  [*] Pickup candidates
  [*] [Gourmand, Artificer] Craft-result prediction
  [*] [Artificer] Remaining usable bomb-jump heat
[/list]

Screen position and slot layout can be customized in Remix.


[h2]Creature labels[/h2]

Puts labels above certain creatures (lizards, scavengers, slugpups), including:
[list]
  [*] Numeric ID
  [*] Recognition name: a name taken from ancient runes
  [*] Intent (off by default): icons for neutral, friendly, and hostile relations
[/list]

Each item can be toggled in Remix.


[h2]Input history[/h2]

A live fighting-game-style log of player inputs and movement states, with frame counts. It records:
[list]
  [*] Movement direction: eight directions + neutral
  [*] Actions: grab, jump, throw, special
  [*] States: standing, crawling, airborne, climbing poles...
[/list]

Remix can configure which items to track.

Movement states and actions use letters by default. You can switch to icons.
(The config panel has a legend. I drew the current icons myself and I am not happy with them; improvements are welcome.)


[h2]Misc[/h2]

Bind keys in [url=https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/?id=3458119961]Improved Input Config[/url] to toggle each panel in-game.
Inventory and creature labels have no default binds. Input history defaults to keyboard [b]H[/b] and gamepad [b]L3[/b].

Multiplayer: local-only. It does not affect online play.
