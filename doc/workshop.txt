添加了多项实用 HUD。

[url=https://github.com/esphas/MoreSlugHUD]Github[/url]


[b]库存[/b]

显示当前库存（双手、腹部、背部）的物品；并显示合成结果预测。

该功能受到 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2957745837]What's in My Pocket?[/url] 启发。
（事实上这个 mod 的起点就是“我想把它待办里说的合成预测做出来”，只不过后续加入了其他新的想法，才导致最终单独写了一个 mod，而不是一个 fork。衷心致谢。）

支持十字和横排两种展示方式，可自定义屏幕坐标。

[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/esphas/MoreSlugHUD/static/preview-inventory-cross.png[/img]
[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/esphas/MoreSlugHUD/static/preview-inventory-row.png[/img]


[b]生物标签[/b]

给特定生物（蜥蜴、拾荒者、猫崽）头顶展示编号、辨识名与意图（中立、友善与攻击）。

该功能受到 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2934997065]Visible ID[/url] 启发。

辨识名：使用古代符文为生物取一个名字；我自己也不知道怎么读但我感觉很酷。
（我本计划允许玩家自定义名称，但在着手开发此功能之前，我注意到 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3775963449]Name Your Friends[/url] 出现了，并且基本能满足我设想的需求；因此最终仅保留了这个用于提供气氛的选项。）

各项目可独立切换是否展示。

[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/esphas/MoreSlugHUD/static/preview-id-1.png[/img]
[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/esphas/MoreSlugHUD/static/preview-id-2.png[/img]


[b]输入历史[/b]

实时展示一个带帧数的玩家输入和运动状态的历史日志。（玩过格斗游戏的话，应该会很熟悉。）

支持在 remix 中详细配置需要展示的内容。

默认使用字母来表示各运动状态和动作，也可切换为图标。
（remix 配置面板中有图例，目前的这套图标是我手绘的，我对其并不满意，欢迎提交改进。）

我在该功能开发完成之后，才注意到类似功能已有 [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3157558337]Debug - Input Log[/url] 提供。经过考察之后，我认为我实现的效果和它提供的仍然存在差异，因此保留了该功能。

[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/esphas/MoreSlugHUD/static/preview-history.png[/img]


[b]杂项[/b]

可在键位设置（Improved Input Config）中配置按键，以在游戏中切换面板的显示状态。
库存、生物标签无默认绑定按键；输入历史默认绑定键盘 [b]H[/b]、手柄 [b]L3[/b]。

联机兼容性：本地模组，不影响联机。

--- en

Several extra HUDs.

[url=https://github.com/esphas/MoreSlugHUD]GitHub[/url]


[b]Inventory[/b]

Shows what you are holding (hands, stomach, back), plus a craft-result preview.

Inspired by [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2957745837]What's in My Pocket?[/url].
(This mod started as "I want to build the craft prediction on their to-do list." Other ideas piled on, so it became its own mod instead of a fork. Many thanks.)

Cross or row layout. You can pick where it sits on screen.

[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/esphas/MoreSlugHUD/static/preview-inventory-cross.png[/img]
[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/esphas/MoreSlugHUD/static/preview-inventory-row.png[/img]


[b]Creature labels[/b]

Puts an ID number, a recognition name, and intent (neutral, friendly, hostile) above lizards, scavengers, and slugpups.

Inspired by [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2934997065]Visible ID[/url].

Recognition names use ancient runes. I cannot read them either, but they look cool.
(I meant to let you rename creatures. Before I started, [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3775963449]Name Your Friends[/url] showed up and already does that job, so this option stayed as flavor only.)

Each piece can be toggled on its own.

[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/esphas/MoreSlugHUD/static/preview-id-1.png[/img]
[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/esphas/MoreSlugHUD/static/preview-id-2.png[/img]


[b]Input history[/b]

A live log of your inputs and movement states, with frame counts. Fighting-game players will know the look.

Remix lets you pick what to show.

Movement states and actions use letters by default. You can switch to icons.
(Remix has a legend. I drew the icons myself and I am not happy with them; better art is welcome.)

I only noticed [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3157558337]Debug - Input Log[/url] after I finished this. I still think the two look different enough, so I kept it.

[img]https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/esphas/MoreSlugHUD/static/preview-history.png[/img]


[b]Misc[/b]

Bind keys in Improved Input Config to toggle each panel in-game.
Inventory and creature labels have no default binds. Input history defaults to keyboard [b]H[/b] and gamepad [b]L3[/b].

Multiplayer: local-only. It does not affect online play.
